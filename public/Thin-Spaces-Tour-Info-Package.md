# Tour Information Package: Thin Spaces and Holy Spaces
## Italy Pilgrimage | April 19 – 29, 2027

Thank you for your interest in the **Thin Spaces and Holy Spaces** tour. This 10-day pilgrimage is designed to be a transformative experience, immersing you in the life and spirituality of St. Francis while exploring some of Italy's most sacred and historic locations.

---

### The Journey
Our route is carefully selected to reflect the path of St. Francis, moving from the heart of the church in Rome to the quiet, contemplative hills of Umbria.

*   **Rome:** Our journey begins in the Eternal City, exploring its ancient roots and significant spiritual landmarks.
*   **Sienna:** We then travel to the medieval city of Sienna, home of St. Catherine, known for its stunning architecture and quiet beauty.
*   **Assisi:** We conclude our pilgrimage in Assisi, the birthplace of St. Francis and St. Clare, where we will spend several days walking the same paths they walked centuries ago.
